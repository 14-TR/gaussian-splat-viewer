Experimental: Create from photos

Turn a small set of overlapping photos into a rough 3D Gaussian scene. Choose 3–6 JPEG or PNG photos of one stationary, textured subject, with closely overlapping views and the same dimensions. Maximum 24 MiB total. Start with three nearby angles; a wide orbit may fail.

Training requires a browser with WebGPU and hardware acceleration. Availability varies by device. Photos are decoded, matched and trained locally in your browser memory; no photos are uploaded. This experiment produces soft, partial scenes and has not been verified on a physical iPhone. The first reconstruction downloads an approximately 46 MiB processing module.

1. Choose your photos

Camera and quality settings

Focal length is measured in pixels at the resized feature-image resolution. It is an approximate camera calibration, not inferred automatically. Use 1,024 px if fine textures do not match; training stays at 256 px. No lens-distortion correction or HEIC support.

2. Reconstruct, then train

Choose photos or try the generated set. Successful camera reconstruction enables training. You can then explore and download the trained PLY.
Diagnostic trainer control

This generated known-camera control supplies exact synthetic geometry to isolate the trainer. It does not reconstruct your photo cameras.

Up to 2,000 Gaussians · 256 px training · 90 s camera / 180 s training budgets. Canceling discards the active job. Your saved PLY remains wherever you download it. Dependency licenses and notices.
